    - Generate IQ Modulation on top of sinewave
    - Apply pulse shaping filters like a Root-Raised Cosine (RRC) filter to limit the signal bandwidth, just like a real transmitter.

2. Channel Effects (The "Real World" Math)
    - AWGN(Additive White Gaussian Noise): Simulate thermal noise at varying SNR levels
    - Carrier Frequency Offset(CFO) & Phase Noise: Add a slight frequency drift to simulate imperfect crystal oscillators in real transmitter & receiver
    - Multpath Fading: Simulate signal bouncing off buildings by adding delayed, attenuated copies of signal (Rayleigh/Rician fading)

3. Receiver DSP

    - Time Synchronization: Algorithms (Gardner/Mueller-Mller) to find exactly where a symbol begins
